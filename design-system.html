01 / foundations

Design principles

Make complexity legible, and system feedback trustworthy.

Context first

Show the reservation, vehicle, and next decision together so operators do not have to reconstruct context.

Tactile clarity

Controls use generous targets, visible state layers, and familiar Material shapes for confident touch.

Trustworthy states

Success, warning, unavailable, and loading states are explicit and never depend on color alone.

06 / accessibility

Accessibility contract

A system is only useful when every operator can read, reach, and understand it.

Keyboard visible

Every interactive element has a 3px high-contrast focus ring with a 3px offset.

Touch ready

Primary controls are at least 44px tall and spacing prevents accidental activation.

Meaningful contrast

Text and semantic controls use dark role colors on light tonal surfaces; status includes icon and label.

Motion aware

Loading and transitions honor prefers-reduced-motion; forced colors remain supported.
